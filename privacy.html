سياسة الخصوصية

آخر تحديث: ٨ أكتوبر ٢٠٢٦

المصطبة بتستخدم خدمات YouTube API عشان تقرا بيانات عامة عن قنوات وحلقات البودكاست، زي العناوين ومدة الحلقات وأعداد المشاهدات والإعجابات والتعليقات.

مفيش حسابات ولا تسجيل دخول، ومش بنجمع بيانات شخصية عن زوار الموقع. لو رشّحت بودكاست من الفورم بتاعنا، بنستخدم البيانات اللي بتكتبها بس عشان نراجع البودكاست ونتواصل معاك. النسخة الإنجليزية تحت هي النص الكامل.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information mastaba.fm ("المصطبة", "we") uses and collects. Last updated 8 October 2026.

YouTube API Services

المصطبة uses YouTube API Services to read public data about podcast channels and videos, including channel names and thumbnails, video titles, durations, publish dates, and view, like and comment counts. We use this data to calculate and publish a weekly chart. We do not access any YouTube account data, and we never ask you to sign in with Google or YouTube.

YouTube API Services are provided by Google. Google's Privacy Policy applies to them: https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you have ever granted any application access to your Google account, you can review or revoke that access at Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. المصطبة does not request such access.

Information about visitors

  • We don't offer accounts or sign-in, and we don't collect names, email addresses or other personal data through this website.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or analytics trackers.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and operations, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which may receive your IP address.

Stored data

We store the public YouTube statistics described above in order to calculate weekly changes and trends. We handle this data in line with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply. We don't sell or share it.

Podcast suggestions

If you suggest a podcast through our Google Form, we use the details you give (the show's name and links, and any contact details you choose to add) only to review and list the show and to contact you about it. We don't sell or share them. The form is hosted by Google, under Google's privacy policy.

Podcast creators

If you run a podcast that appears on المصطبة and have a question about your listing, email us and we'll respond.

Changes and contact

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes. Questions: hello@mastaba.fm. mastaba.fm is operated from Cairo, Egypt.
