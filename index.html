About المصطبة

المصطبة (mastaba.fm) is a weekly public chart of Arabic-language podcasts published on YouTube, starting with Egypt. The name means the bench outside a village house where people gather in the evening to talk.

We use the YouTube Data API to read public information about podcast channels and episodes: titles, durations, publish dates, and view, like and comment counts. From daily readings we calculate how many views each show's recent episodes gained during the week, and publish a ranked chart. Every entry links back to the show on YouTube.

View counts come from YouTube. Rankings, weekly gains and scores are our own calculations and are not YouTube metrics. المصطبة is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube. No one can pay to appear on the chart or to rank higher.

We don't offer accounts or sign-in, and we don't collect personal data from visitors. The first chart has not been published yet; the example above uses fictional names and numbers.
