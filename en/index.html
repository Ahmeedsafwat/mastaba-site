How we count

The views come from YouTube; the ranking is ours. These are all the rules.

  1. 1

    Every day

    We record the views, likes and comments of every episode. YouTube only shows you today's number, so we record it every day to work out the week.

  2. 2

    At the end of the week

    We work out how many views each episode gained during the week.

  3. 3

    The ranking

    From those numbers we make two boards: the Top 20 and Rising.

Top 20 who grew most

We rank shows by the views gained this week on their full episodes (20 minutes or more) published in the last 90 days. Clips don't count.

An episode older than 90 days drops out of the count, so a show can fall in the ranking even if its numbers didn't go down.

Rising against itself

Here we compare every show with itself: this week against its usual. A small show having its best week can come ahead of a big show having an ordinary one.

The Top 20 is different: it counts the views as they are.

Who gets on the chart

  • A conversation between two or more peopleNot one person talking alone.
  • Episodes of 20 minutes or moreFull episodes only, not clips.
  • At least 3 episodes in the last 90 daysSo the show is still running.
No paid placement

No one can pay to get on the chart or to move up it.

We only claim what we count

We count YouTube views, and we don't say who is most listened to. A show that doesn't publish its full episodes on YouTube won't appear on the chart.

If we change a rule, we'll say so

We'll write the change on the rules page before we apply it.

About mastaba.fm

المصطبة (el-mastaba) is the stone bench outside a village house in Egypt, where people sit in the evening and talk. That's what a podcast is, so that's what we named the chart.

mastaba.fm is a weekly chart of Arabic podcasts on YouTube, starting with Egypt. Every day we read public data about podcast channels and episodes through the YouTube Data API: titles, durations, publish dates, and view, like and comment counts. From those daily readings we work out how many views each show's recent episodes gained during the week, and rank the shows.

The view counts come from YouTube. The rankings, weekly gains and any scores are our own calculations, not YouTube metrics. We're independent: mastaba.fm is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google, we don't produce or own any show, and no one can pay to appear on the chart or to move up it.

There's nothing to sign up for. The site has no accounts or sign-in and doesn't collect personal data from visitors. We've been collecting data since 19 September 2026 and track more than 300 shows. The latest chart is at mastaba.fm/en/chart/2026-11-15/, and every chart is listed at mastaba.fm/en/charts/.
